# Barcelona first slice: historical basis

Setting: 19 July 1936, the south-west approach to Plaça de Catalunya and the upper Rambla. Rebel army units fought workers and forces loyal to the Republic. The Hotel Colón was a rebel stronghold around the square.

Sources checked for this slice:

- Museu Nacional d’Art de Catalunya, *The Hotel Colón: the story behind a snapshot by Antoni Campañà*: https://blog.museunacional.cat/en/the-hotel-colon-the-story-behind-a-snapshot-by-antoni-campana/ . Supports the square/Hotel Colón setting and distinguishes the uprising from the later occupation of the building. The later PSUC and Lenin/Stalin banners are deliberately absent.
- Barcelona memory sites: https://memoriabcn.cat/espais-memoria/ . Documents the 19 July military uprising, resistance by workers and loyal forces, and acquisition of weapons at Drassanes. The fictional contact in this slice is not a claim that a documented arms distribution happened at this particular printer.
- Arxiu Nacional de Catalunya, teaching source with troop-movement map: https://static.arxiusenlinia.cultura.gencat.cat/active/GANC/TEXTUAL/1/10/0/51/7307059_1019507%2C0003.pdf . Used for the city-wide context, not exact battle placement.

The map compresses distances, footprints and connecting streets for play. Buildings suggest the period skyline rather than reproduce architectural plans. Tram rails, shops and apartment balconies are environmental approximations. Soldiers wear generic khaki clothing and caps without a claim about a specific regiment. The four playable neighbours, local contact, friendly fighters, patrol, material piles, barricade and attack timings are fictional. The conclusion describes holding one position while fighting continues elsewhere; it does not credit this group with defeating the uprising.
