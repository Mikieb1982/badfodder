CABLE STREET

LONDON · 4 OCTOBER 1936

GROUNDWORK · MAP RECONSTRUCTION PENDING

A separate historical mission built around maintaining obstructions, assisting residents, carrying materials and managing withdrawal rather than a standard military firefight.

  1. GatheringInspect the defence, deliver material and assist a resident.
  2. Hold the approachMaintain the forward obstruction while the principal defence is prepared.
  3. RegroupFall back, assist civilians and restore support access.
  4. They Shall Not PassKeep the final route blocked until the hold is complete.

The mechanics framework is now in development. Playable street geometry will only be added after the period map has been verified.
